Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tokens actually usable?
Yes. Flash USDT functions exactly like regular USDT on the blockchain. You can deposit them into Binance, Kraken, or other exchanges. They are only "flash" because they will expire after 90 days if not moved to a main exchange that validates reserves. For 90 days, they are fully spendable and tradable.
Why is the price lower than market rate?
Because we don't hold the underlying reserve collateral indefinitely. We provide you with the liquidity you need for a specific window (90 days) at a discount. You get the appearance of wealth without tying up your actual capital for years.
How long does delivery take?
Once your payment is confirmed on the blockchain (usually 1-5 minutes), our system automatically triggers the transfer. You will receive your Flash USDT in your wallet within 10 minutes.
What happens after 90 days?
The tokens will become invalid if they remain in a private wallet. If you move them to a major exchange (like Binance) within the 90-day window, they often convert to regular USDT. Check your specific exchange's policy on Flash Token acceptance.
